EXPERIMENT 1✈️ Wing Size — the Fold-Line Trick

When you fold the wings (Step 5), where the fold line goes decides how big the wings are. Fold line hugging the body → big wings. Fold line far from the body → small wings, chunky body. Slide it and watch the finished plane change:

EXPERIMENT 2📎 Paperclip & the Balance Point

Every plane balances on one point — its center of gravity (the glowing orange dot). A paperclip moves it. Pick a spot and see where the dot goes:

FLIGHT LOG📋 Record Every Flight

  • Same start line every time — stand on a piece of tape on the floor.
  • Same throw: gentle, aiming slightly up. Let the plane do the flying — don't hurl it.
  • 3 flights per version. Measure each flight in steps: walk from the start line to where the plane's nose stopped and count your steps. Same shoes, same stride every time — that is what makes it science. Write the step counts down right away.
  • One change at a time. Two changes = mystery data.
